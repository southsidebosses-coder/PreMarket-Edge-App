Privacy Policy — PreMarket Edge & Position Sizer

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE] · Android · v1.0

1. Overview

PreMarket Edge & Position Sizer is an offline-first trading calculator and journal. We do not operate accounts, and we never see your trading data. The only third party that processes user data through this app is Google's advertising service, described in §3.

2. Data stored on your device (never transmitted)

Your setup journal, risk-parameter defaults, and the ad-free purchase flag are stored locally using the device's AsyncStorage. They are not uploaded to us or anyone else, and they are permanently erased when you uninstall the app or use the journal's CLEAR ALL.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner ads and a limited interstitial ad via the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). For this the SDK collects and shares with Google: your advertising identifier (device or other IDs) and approximate location derived from IP signals, plus basic ad-interaction events — for advertising and measurement purposes. The app itself requests no location permissions and never reads GPS.

  • Google's use of this data: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Opt out of ads personalization: device Settings → Google → Ads, or adssettings.google.com
  • Remove all ads at any time via the one-time in-app upgrade.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. The only sharing is app data ↔ Google AdMob as described in §3. There are no other third-party SDKs, trackers, or analytics.

5. Retention & deletion

Local data lives on your device until you delete it (uninstall or CLEAR ALL). For data processed by Google through ads, use the Google links in §3, or contact us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] and we will assist.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not marketed to them. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Permissions

Internet access only (for market data and ads). No camera, location, contacts, storage, or phone-state permissions are requested.

8. Financial disclaimer

PreMarket Edge is an analytical tool and trading journal — not financial advice. No output is a recommendation. Trading options involves substantial risk of loss.
